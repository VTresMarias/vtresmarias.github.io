<h1>
  VTresMarias announces first-ever Fortification of Blessing for organization
  "Samahan ng Puso"
</h1>
<span>published 10 Dec 2024</span>
<br><br>
<p>
  VTresMarias has announced the first-ever blessing of an organization that
  fortifies the Marias' Core Values for the benefit of all!!
</p>
<br>
<p>
  Samahan Ng Puso, an initiative led by the Fourth CosMaria -- Shunni/Shūnni Cos
  -- supports the lives of Bataeños across rural areas who do not have access to
  their basic necessities.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  their efforts constitute to strengthening the Marias' Core Values by remaining
  open-hearted to those who needed them the most, and to admit one's flaws
  without fear of discrimination.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  the fortification of this blessing will take place on 22 Dec 2024, with the
  First Maria🍃 physically present for the occasion.
</p>
