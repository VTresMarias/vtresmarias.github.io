<h1>uplifting the Marias' Core Values through cosplay</h1>
<span>published 5 Jun 2025</span>
<br><br>
<p>
  VTresMarias prides itself as a sole VTuber Collective that strives for the
  greater good through its Core Values. however, in order to enact on it, the
  Collective has to be involved in areas of interest that share the same value
  -- and fortify them in an equal and accessible manner.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  with the debut of its first Sub-Collective -- CosMarias -- the Collective
  understands that belief. in addition, this move aids to recognize the
  importance that comes with dedication and inspiration blooming from within.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  furthermore, the First Maria🍃's involvement in such field provided a catalyst
  for universality across cosplayers and cosplay in general.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  hence, the Marias have seen fit to officially provide its blessing to two
  organizations that made each moment count.
</p>
<br><br>
<h2>a word from the First Maria🍃</h2>
<br>
<p>
  since officially debuting CosMarias last 30 Dec 2024, I have been consistently
  getting involved in cosplay conventions, hoping that the message of
  "Universality" and "Acceptance" may spread across the community and beyond --
  no matter the circumstances.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  and in those times, I have found my solace in such conventions -- often
  through presences with other cosplayers who believed in the same value and
  virtue as I do.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  but most of all, I have met the organizers who strive to provide the same kind
  of value, undermining the circumstances brought upon by their efforts behind
  the scenes.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  as the First Maria🍃, it has seen fit to officially induct two of those
  organizers who believe and share the same passion as I do -- among other
  organizations that share the same values as the Marias.
</p>
<br>
<p>you may learn more about them through the following below:</p>
<br><br>
<h2>meet the Inductees</h2>
<br>
<h3>Connxt: Anime Popculture Event × Arterion Philippines</h3>
<br>
<p>
  you may have heard of Connxt some several months back -- they were the ones
  who have successfully hosted the Gakuen-Sai last 15-16 Sep 2024 (previously
  known as the Balanga Leg Special). their dedication to provide meaningful
  experiences through the world of cosplay has made them one of the prominent
  organizers in Central Luzon.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  since their merge with Arterion Philippines (which was officiated during Yoru
  Matsuri last 24 May 2025), they have promised to strive even more to provide
  the cosplayers and the audience with an experience they will never forget.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  the First Maria🍃 has appeared in those events in form of a participation,
  which is also a part of her evaluation to see if they have performed well. all
  considerations at hand, they have proven to have more than what they have
  offered to the masses.
</p>

</p>
