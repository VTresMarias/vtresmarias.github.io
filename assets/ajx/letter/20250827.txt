<h1>I don't wanna hear your excuses.</h1>
<span>published 27 Aug 2025</span>
<br><br>
<p><i>
  this will serve as the initial open letter to Israel's Prime Minister,
  Benjamin Netanyahu.
</i></p>
<br>
<p>
  despite the pleas from the public and the institutions, you still remain
  committed to reclaim what is yours and Israel's by any means necessary -- even
  if it means destroying everything that's in your way.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  but your way of doing things has become a problem at large, and trust me; it's
  not the Gazans that are devastated by your show of antics. you have angered
  the communities that only strive to live in peace, health workers -- and
  worse, the Members of the Press.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  it has come to the Marias' attention that the recent bombings that made over a
  Gazan hospital at Khan Yunis has claimed the lives of 5 journalists who worked
  for respectable news institutions, including Reuters and Al Jazeera.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  and based on the reports that we have received from various news outlets, you
  have claimed that the so-called "incident" is just a "tragic mishap".
</p>
<br>
<p>I call bullshit.</p>
<br>
<p>
  we have seen your excuses in full view, even going as far as to suppress
  independent and reputable news outlets that you claimed that are against you
  and your regime -- all for your sake of security of your country.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  just how many times will you try to at <i>*least*</i> admit that you led your
  IDF to the killing of the journalists who want to get the truth out about the
  situation, and innocent people who really want to help those in need -- all
  without being accused of being affiliated with Hamas in the first place??!
</p>
<br>
<p>
  if you keep this up -- in which we know you absolutely will -- we at
  VTresMarias will be publishing a Press Release officially denouncing your
  presence across our institution.
</p>
