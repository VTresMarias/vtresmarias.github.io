<h1>コスプレのマリア: Synopsis</h1>
<br>
<p>
  three days after VTresMarias was officially unveiled to the world, the First
  Maria🍃 sets on her first mission: to determine which area of interest suits
  the Collective's Core Values and aim to maintain their presence through
  various of ways.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  it just so happened that her primary interest beyond this is through a world
  of cosplay, to which she was introduced to a few months prior.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  with her unwitting determination to do something extra-special, she sets on a
  journey and try to delve as much as she can -- which turned out to be more
  fruitful than what needs to be. in fact, she has made friends throughout her
  first attempt, that it had inspired her to do it over again.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  many months have passed, and her desire to continue doing this venture is
  constantly increasing; that it had earned her place in some cosplay
  communities to which she belonged to. and all the while, she has met a number
  of new friends along the way -- with some of them resonating the same value as
  the Marias.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  through this disposition, she has been bestowed a new mission: form a
  Collective which aims to strengthen the Core Values, and make their hobbies
  known to more areas.
</p>
<br>
<p>
  serving her duty as the incumbent First CosMaria, her job has just begun to
  bear fruit..
</p>
