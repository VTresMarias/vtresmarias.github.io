<h1>Chapter 9: Memories</h1>
<br>
<p inter>A day later…</p>
<p>
  I made my way towards the place where Hina and I were supposed to meet -- one
  of her gardens. “Where could she be?” I thought.
</p>
<p>
  From afar, I can see Hina panting as she runs in my direction. “Sorry I’m
  late,” she said.
</p>
<p>
  “There’s nothing to apologize for,” I hushed it off. “Rather, why did you take
  me to this place?”
</p>
<p>
  Regaining her breath, she calmly walks down towards a row full of young
  plants. “Here,” she said, handing me a planter. “This is one of the saplings
  of the Cherry Blossom. Someone gave a whole bag of seeds and this one to me
  some time ago.”
</p>
<p>“That looks beautiful,” I gleam at the sapling. “Where are the others?”</p>
<p>
  “They’re right over there,” she glances at the bench in response, where a
  number of planters and their saplings of a similar kind were placed.
</p>
<p>“Wow.”</p>
<p>
  Carefully placing the planter back to its original spot, Hina slowly holds my
  hand. “You know, I wanted to ask you a little question so I decided that we
  meet up here.”
</p>
<p>“Why? You could ask me some in your room, though.”</p>
<p>
  “It’s tranquil here,” she said with a slow nod. “I like the atmosphere of this
  place, it makes me wanna read a book without dealing with the outside noise.
  And the planters that we saw had a significance to it.”
</p>
<p>I nod. “Okay, what was it?”</p>
<p>
  “So… yesterday, I found a box on top of one of the drawers. They contain three
  bracelets, a photo frame, and three pins of varying symbols.”
</p>
<p>“A… box?”</p>
<p>
  “It’s not just an ordinary box, as far as I can tell,” she shakes her head.
  “So I had to ask the Grand Lady about it. She told me that all will be in
  place when the so-called ‘First Maria’ will then be able to rekindle my heart
  once more.”
</p>
<p>“Sounds like a dream, if you ask me.”</p>
<p>“But the question is… who’s the First Maria?”</p>
<p>
  I shrug. “I have no idea. Whatever that was, though, might give us a clue on
  what’s going on.”
</p>
<p>“Yeah, I could bet on that part.”</p>
<p>
  I think for a moment. “Hina may be right about the ‘First Maria’ thing, but…
  how did I put myself into that picture? I’m sure only the Grand Lady knows
  what was going on.”
</p>
<p>…</p>
<p inter>Back at the castle…</p>
<p>
  I was already in her room together with the Oujo-sama -- Hina. She appears to
  be distraught, and in some sort of confused state that I had to calm her down
  every few minutes. “Is everything alright?”
</p>
<p>“I’m good,” she said, calming down for a bit.</p>
<p>Immediately, I pour a glass of water. “Here, drink this.”</p>
<p>“Thanks,” she said as she gulped down the water. “I’m a little okay now.”</p>
<p>
  “But, you need to rest,” I declare. “You’re not in a proper state to do
  anything, and we also need to prepare for your Coronation soon.”
</p>
<p>“Yeah, you’re right. But…”</p>
<p>
  What came afterwards was a question that I didn’t expect to hear coming from
  her. Is she okay to ask me such a huge favor?
</p>
