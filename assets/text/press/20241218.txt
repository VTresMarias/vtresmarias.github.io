<h1>our stance with involved persons/organizations moving forward</h1>
<span>published 18 Dec 2024</span>
<br>
<p>
  it has come to our attention that there have been reports about some persons
  or organizations that wanted to leverage the kindness that the our Members of
  the Collective has shown that we felt was deeply questionable.
</p>
<p>
  although we are not naming such persons/organizations as this will only be
  serving as a general reminder, however,
  <i
    >we are taking these reports seriously, as it not only impacts the general
    status of the Collective, but also within the Members themselves.
  </i>
</p>
<p>
  aligned with this, the First Maria🍃 has issued an
  <b>executive decision</b> on behalf of the Collective and its Sub-Collectives,
  which is described as follows:
</p>
<br>
<div style="text-align: center">
  <h3>
    VTresMarias and its associated Collectives (Sub-Collectives) will no longer
    support persons or organizations that speak/act with ill will against
    Members of the Collective.
  </h3>
  <h3>
    in addition, persons/organizations found to have violated this disposition
    will have their privileges revoked, including the nullification of their
    recognition/s from our systems.
  </h3>
  <h3>effective immediately.</h3>
</div>
<br>
<p>
  we will be opening our dedicated email address in order to cater to the
  inquiries brought upon by this move. it should be expected to debut during the
  2nd Anniversary of the Marias -- 27 Dec 2024.
</p>
