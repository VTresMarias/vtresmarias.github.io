celebrating 3 years of becoming one with the Marias


VTresMarias has come a long way since its inception on 27 Dec 2022, pursuing the Core Values for the betterment of the VTuber Community and beyond. and along the way, it became more than just a VTuber Collective -- it's a culmination of ideas bringing together to form a community that has uplifted the spirits of not just the people around them, but also themselves.


as the Marias continue ushering on to further horizons -- with the First Maria🍃 at the helm of it all -- they continue to press on with their mission to bring joy and comfort to those who need it the most, no matter the circumstances it may bring.

the Marias' Core Values

一味

Universality


the Marias' main focus is to wholeheartedly recognize people regardless of who they are, without any fear of discrimination and oppression from all parties.

了承

Acceptance


the Collective's stance stems from the genuine love of one, where a love can become the driving force in making the world a better place for everyone.

enacting on these approaches require evaluation from all parties to ensure that the Communities involved remain peaceful, even in trying times.

synopsis


“Mother loves you, always.”


Mother Agatha was once a beloved Mother whose beauty is unmatched, until she succumbed to the forces of evil due to hunger in a never-ending winter. She had sacrificed three of her children for food, but her fate had been sealed by which her youngest son wanted to seek the truth and give justice to his lost siblings.


But when her story comes to a close, another one opens -- a light in her midst of darkness, with a mission that can reshape her destiny anew.


This is a story of how a Mother once lost everything, but has made a new life with those who cared about her the most. The story of the First Maria -- Mother Agatha, now known as 💚🍃Aga-chuu💗🌸.
