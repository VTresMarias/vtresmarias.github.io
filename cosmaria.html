“the integrity of the Sisterhood is vital to the integrity of all.”

as VTresMarias' first Sub-Collective, the Cosplay Marias (also known as CosMarias or CSM) aim to expand the Marias' Core Values to the cosplay community and many areas of interest.


handpicked and supported by the First Maria🍃 (performing her duties as the Incumbent First CosMaria🍃💐), the Sub-Collective's members embrace the Marias' duties by showcasing their best efforts to their audience.

explore the stories of CSM

chapter 0: the Sisterhood

unveiled 15 Mar 2025

three days after VTresMarias was officially unveiled to the world, the First Maria🍃 sets on her first mission: to determine which area of interest suits the Collective's Core Values and aim to maintain their presence through various of ways.


it just so happened that her primary interest beyond this is through a world of cosplay, to which she was introduced to a few months prior.


with her unwitting determination to do something extra-special, she sets on a journey and try to delve as much as she can -- which turned out to be more fruitful than what needs to be. in fact, she has made friends throughout her first attempt, that it had inspired her to do it over again.


many months have passed, and her desire to continue doing this venture is constantly increasing; that it had earned her place in some cosplay communities to which she belonged to. and all the while, she has met a number of new friends along the way -- with some of them resonating the same value as the Marias.


through this disposition, she has been bestowed a new mission: form a Collective which aims to strengthen the Core Values, and make their hobbies known to more areas.


serving her duty as the Incumbent First CosMaria🍃💐, her job has just begun to bear fruit..



chapter 1: the Forthcoming

unveiled 27 Dec 2025

many days came and went, and the First Maria🍃 -- fulfilling her duties as the Incumbent First CosMaria🍃💐 -- has been constantly connecting with everyone across the cosplay community; whether it would be around her area and beyond.


through her efforts (and those around her), many people begin to understand the importance of cosplay as a way to express oneself -- and how it can be a way to spread positivity to others.


together with a new blessing from the Grand Lady🌙, she's more than ready to accept the fact that her 'motherly' figure is now being put to the test -- as she is to welcome new members that she will be taking care of for many years to come..
